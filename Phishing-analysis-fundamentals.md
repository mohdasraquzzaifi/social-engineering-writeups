# Phishing Analysis Fundamentals – TryHackMe Write-up

## Introduction
Phishing is a form of social engineering where attackers attempt to trick users into revealing sensitive information such as passwords or financial details. This exercise focuses on understanding how phishing emails are structured and how to identify them.

## Room Overview
The Phishing Analysis Fundamentals room introduces the core components of phishing emails. It explains how attackers manipulate email headers, sender addresses, and content to appear legitimate.

## What I Learned
- How phishing emails spoof sender addresses
- The role of malicious links and attachments
- Why attackers create urgency or fear

## Red Flags Identified
- Suspicious sender domains
- Unexpected requests for sensitive information
- Poor grammar or spelling
- Mismatched links

## Key Takeaways
This exercise helped me understand how to analyze emails carefully before clicking links or downloading files. Verifying the sender and inspecting URLs are essential skills for staying safe online.

## Conclusion
Phishing remains one of the most common cyber threats. Learning how to identify phishing emails is critical for both personal and professional security.
