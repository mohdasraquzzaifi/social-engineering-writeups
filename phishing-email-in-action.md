# Phishing Emails in Action – TryHackMe Write-up

## Introduction
Phishing emails often mimic trusted organizations to deceive users. This exercise focuses on analyzing real-world phishing email examples.

## Room Overview
The Phishing Emails in Action room presents realistic phishing attempts and teaches how to recognize malicious patterns.

## What I Learned
- How attackers disguise malicious links
- How urgency is used to pressure users
- Common phishing email structures

## Red Flags Identified
- Generic greetings
- Suspicious attachments
- Fake branding
- Requests for login or payment details

## Key Takeaways
Careful email inspection can prevent data theft and malware infections. This exercise reinforced the importance of skepticism when receiving unexpected emails.

## Conclusion
Understanding phishing tactics is a key defensive skill in cybersecurity. This room provided hands-on experience in phishing detection.
